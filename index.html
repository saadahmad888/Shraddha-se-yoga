Your teacher · गुरु

Shraddha means
faith.

I teach the way I wish someone had taught me: slowly, in plain language, and starting from the body you actually have today — not the one in the photograph.

Most people arrive with the same three things. A back that complains by Wednesday. Sleep that never quite lands. And a head that will not switch off. We begin with one conversation about which of those is loudest, then build a short daily practice you'll actually keep.
